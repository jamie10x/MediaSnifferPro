Stream downloading

Where the desktop helper saves files. Browser (in-page) downloads always go to your browser's Downloads folder.

Simple clear HLS (MPEG-TS) streams are downloaded and remuxed to MP4 in your browser via mux.js. Complex streams (fMP4, DASH, large or long downloads) are handed to the desktop helper, which uses real ffmpeg.
